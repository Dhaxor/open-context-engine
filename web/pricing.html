How activation works

Your order email contains a license key. Run oce activate polar_lk_… — we verify it with Polar once and issue a signed token that checks out locally against an embedded public key from then on. No account to create, and it keeps working air-gapped.

Why per-seat is honest here

Seats are in the signed license and enforcement is honor-based by design — your procurement can audit it in the source. We'd rather be verifiable than adversarial.
